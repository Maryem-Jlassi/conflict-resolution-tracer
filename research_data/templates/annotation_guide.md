# Blind annotation guide

Annotate from claims, evidence, and temporal validity only. Do not seek or infer system outputs or hypotheses.

- `existing`: only the incumbent is supported at the relevant time.
- `incoming`: only the incoming claim is supported.
- `both_compatible`: both can simultaneously be true; neither should overwrite the other.
- `both_wrong`: evidence independently contradicts both.
- `unresolved`: evidence is insufficient, ambiguous, contradictory, or requires expertise unavailable to the annotator.

Time-dependent truth is judged at the case’s declared event and validity interval, not at annotation time. Expected unresolved cases should remain unresolved rather than forced into a winner. Escalate when evidence authenticity is uncertain, validity intervals conflict, specialist knowledge is required, or annotators disagree after independent review.
