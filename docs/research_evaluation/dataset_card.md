# Dataset Card

No final held-out research dataset is installed. The 16+3 cases are a frozen pipeline-debugging pilot. Corrected Benchmark D and synthetic Benchmarks A–E are diagnostic or engineering evidence and cannot support generalization claims.

The final dataset must record origin, license, collection date, immutable item IDs, split assignment, deduplication, domain distribution, construction, labeling instructions, independent adjudicators, disagreement resolution, and exclusions. Ground truth must be independent of evaluated agents and the LCM decision. Secrets and identifying data are prohibited. Dataset version and manifest hash are mandatory.
