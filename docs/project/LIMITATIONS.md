# Limitations

LCM is a research prototype, not a production-certified memory, security, medical, legal, or financial system.

- Final empirical validation is incomplete; pilot annotation is pending.
- Included synthetic benchmarks are diagnostic and may encode their expected behavior by construction.
- Operational real-agent execution does not demonstrate correctness without independently adjudicated ground truth.
- Equal Ψ weights are a symmetry prior, not an optimized or externally validated configuration.
- Trust depends on verification quality and independence; correlated or erroneous feedback can bias it.
- Temporal decay and provenance policies require domain-specific review.
- External LongMemEval and LoCoMo data are not redistributed here.
- No frozen-test labels, final comparisons, or headline accuracy figures are included.
- Security controls have engineering tests but have not undergone an independent production security audit.

See the preregistered methodology under `docs/research_evaluation/` for the planned evaluation boundaries.
